# The Hobbit illustration prompts

The built-in image generator produced the chapter plates. The shared art direction was traditional storybook realism in textured gouache, atmospheric light, landscape format and full-bleed compositions. The numbered subjects correspond to the JPEG filenames.

1. A wide, traditional painted fantasy chapter plate for a family read-aloud edition of The Hobbit. Bilbo, a barefoot, curly chestnut-haired middle-aged hobbit in a green waistcoat, looks across his crowded oak supper table at a blue-hooded dwarf softly playing a golden harp. A round green door, a small teapot, lamplight in warm Bag End, Gandalf listening in shadow. Expressive faces, textured gouache, richly detailed storybook realism, calm, child-friendly, no text, frame, or lettering.
2. Three lumbering stone trolls frozen forever around the cold embers of a campfire in a clearing at sunrise. A curious small hobbit peers from behind a tree. Misty woods.
3. A close study of an old, thin folded parchment map resting on a stone windowsill. A silver crescent moon shines through the paper so a few pale decorative runic marks show. Beyond, a green valley at night and a little waterfall. No people.
4. Blue-white lightning lights the jagged Misty Mountains. A little line of ponies, dwarves in wet hoods and a hobbit hurry along a high ledge towards a cave opening, seen from a safe distance.
5. Riddles by an underground lake. Bilbo stands on the stony shore with a little blue-glowing blade held low, facing pale thin Gollum's enormous eyes as Gollum sits in a tiny boat. Reflective dark water, wonder and suspense.
6. A magnificent golden eagle soars from the tops of smoke-wreathed pine trees with a bearded dwarf in its talons, a small hobbit clinging to the dwarf's legs. Night sky and moon.
7. Beorn, a very tall broad-shouldered man with thick dark hair and beard in a rough wool tunic, welcomes two tiny travellers at the gate to his timber hall. Giant bees, garden of flowers and beehives in morning sun.
8. Bilbo in his green waistcoat parts the upper leaves of Mirkwood and lifts his face above a sea of treetops into golden light and fluttering black butterflies. Strong sense of height and relief.
9. Bilbo clinging to a closed wooden barrel among many sealed oak barrels floating under branches on a river just outside the woodland elf king's water gate, dawn.
10. A welcoming lake town of steep-roofed medieval timber houses on stilts, seen from a small wooden quay. Warm lantern reflections in blue evening water, a distant solitary mountain, a few boats tied up. A peaceful architectural landscape, no figures.
11. At the end of day on a mountain ledge a tiny grey thrush taps a snail on stone. One narrow ray of sunset illuminates a small keyhole in a wall of grey rock. Bilbo points, and a dwarf holds a heavy iron key.
12. The enormous red-gold dragon Smaug reclines upon coins in a vast dwarven treasury. Regal head in three-quarter view, one wise golden eye half open. A tiny two-handled gold cup in the foreground. No hobbit visible.
13. Bilbo, carrying a small torch, finds a milky white gem that shines in many colours deep in the abandoned treasure hall of Erebor. Gold and blue shadows, the stone held in his hand.
14. A thrush resting on a wooden mooring post at the edge of a wide lake. In the far distance on the bank, families under blankets gather around a warm cooking pot, giving one another shelter. Dawn, compassionate and peaceful, no weapons or fighting.
15. A wise old grey raven perched on a newly built low stone wall at a mountain entrance. A few little canvas tents far below in an autumn valley. Mountain wind ruffles its feathers. No people.
16. Bilbo, small hobbit in green waistcoat, quietly offers a shining white jewel to a grave dark-haired man and a slender elven king crowned with autumn leaves in a lamplit campaign tent at night.
17. From the mountain spur Bilbo spots the great eagles circling above distant banners as storm clouds break. A panoramic mountain valley, a glint of sunlight on feathers, battle only suggested in distance.
18. A quiet farewell inside a mountain tent. Bilbo sits holding the hand of his old friend Thorin, a grey-bearded dwarf resting beneath a blue blanket. Even light on kind faces, grief and affection, no wounds visible.
19. Bilbo returns to his sunny round green door and finds an auction of his own belongings in progress on the grass, other hobbits carrying chairs and a clock. His astonishment gives the scene a gentle humour.

The new portraits of Thorin and Balin come from plate 1, Beorn from plate 7, Smaug from plate 12, and Bard and the Elvenking from plate 16.
